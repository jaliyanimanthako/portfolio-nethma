StPageFlip v2.0.0 — local hardcover positioning correction

The original HTML hardcover renderer uses a vertical translation of zero,
while stationary pages use the centered book rectangle's top coordinate.
Hovering a cover corner or starting a cover turn therefore moves the cover
to the top of the container. The two hardcover shadows have the same issue.

Corrections in page-flip.browser.js:
- drawHard: use the book rectangle's top for both cover orientations.
- drawHard: also use the book rectangle's left for left-oriented pages.
- drawHardInnerShadow / drawHardOuterShadow: set top to the book rectangle top.
